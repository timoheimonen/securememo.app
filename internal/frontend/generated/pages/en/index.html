How it works

    Write in your browser

    Your memo is encrypted on your device before it ever reaches us. We can’t read it.

  2. 2

    Share link and password separately

  3. 3

    Deleted after read or expiry

    After a successful read, the browser asks the server to delete the memo. Expired memos are cleaned up automatically.

Private by design

Memos often hold the most sensitive things you share. securememo.app is built so that the server cannot read them, and it keeps as little as possible for as short a time as possible.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about how your memo is protected.

Can securememo.app read my memo?

No. Your memo is encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM before upload, using a random password that never leaves your device. The server receives only ciphertext that it cannot decrypt.

Why should the link and the password be sent separately?

Anyone who has both can open the memo. If you send the link by email and the password by text message, for example, one intercepted message is not enough.

What happens after the memo is read?

When the memo decrypts successfully, the recipient's browser asks the server to delete it. A memo that is never read expires after the time you choose, from 8 hours to 2 weeks, and is then removed.

Can I delete a memo I have already sent?

Yes. Together with the link and the password you get a private revoke link. Opening it lets you delete the memo before it has been read.

What if the password is lost?

Then the memo cannot be opened. The password is never sent to our servers, so nobody can recover it. Simply create a new memo.

Does it cost anything or need an account?

No. securememo.app is free, needs no account and uses no cookies, ads or analytics.
